## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does billing work?
You purchase credits which can be used to deploy and maintain servers. Server costs are calculated based on RAM allocation and any enabled backup storage.

### Are there any minimum commitments?
No. You only pay for what you use, when you use it.

### Do you offer refunds?
Refund requests are handled on a case-by-case basis. Contact us at contact@carbon.host for assistance.

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards and PayPal through our secure payment processor.
